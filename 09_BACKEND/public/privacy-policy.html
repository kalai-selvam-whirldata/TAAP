1Information We Collect

What we collect depends on whether you're using TAAP as a therapist or a client, and how you use the platform.

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, if provided
  • Account type (therapist or client)
  • Authentication and account security information

Therapist / Professional Information

  • Professional profile information
  • Practice information
  • Client invitations you send
  • Activities you assign
  • Appointment-related information you manage

Client Information

Depending on how your therapist has set up your account, this may include:

  • Account and profile information
  • Assigned activities
  • Activity completion status
  • Reflections you choose to record
  • Messages or questions sent through TAAP, where enabled
  • Appointment information
  • Information you voluntarily submit through TAAP

Technical Information

  • Device information
  • Browser information
  • IP address
  • Login and security logs
  • App interaction information
  • Cookie information (see "Cookies" below)

Review note: the categories above reflect TAAP's current documented feature set. Engineering/legal should confirm no fields are missing or overstated before this page goes live.

2How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide TAAP's core functionality
  • Connect therapists and their clients within the platform
  • Deliver assigned activities and track completion
  • Support engagement between sessions
  • Manage appointments
  • Provide notifications and reminders
  • Maintain accounts
  • Provide customer support
  • Support security and fraud prevention
  • Improve product reliability and performance
  • Meet applicable legal obligations

We do not use therapy-related or health-related information for advertising or marketing purposes. Any product analytics used to improve TAAP are kept separate from the operational use of your therapy-related data.

4Health Information

Health information deserves additional protection.

TAAP may hold sensitive health- or therapy-related information — activity responses, reflections, shared notes. Where applicable, we handle it separately from ordinary account or marketing data.

Whether information counts as Protected Health Information ("PHI") under HIPAA, or an equivalent elsewhere, depends on the circumstances and entities involved — not everything in TAAP is automatically PHI.

5HIPAA & Protected Health Information

HIPAA applies to certain healthcare providers, health plans, and clearinghouses ("Covered Entities"), and to service providers handling PHI on their behalf ("Business Associates").

If TAAP handles PHI on behalf of a Covered Entity, additional HIPAA obligations may apply. Where required and agreed, TAAP may enter into a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with that entity.

HIPAA does not automatically apply to every health or wellness application, or to every piece of information entered into an application.

6Data Sharing

We may share information with:

  • The therapist or client counterpart within your own TAAP relationship
  • Service providers who help us operate TAAP
  • Infrastructure and hosting providers
  • Authentication providers
  • Communications providers (for example, to deliver notifications)
  • Professional advisers, such as legal or accounting advisers
  • Legal or regulatory authorities, where required by law

We do not sell sensitive therapy or health information for targeted advertising.

7Data Security

Security is part of the architecture.

We apply safeguards intended to protect information on TAAP, including:

  • Encryption
  • Access controls
  • Authentication
  • Role-based permissions
  • Monitoring and logging
  • Backup and recovery practices
  • Secure development practices
  • Vendor risk management

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

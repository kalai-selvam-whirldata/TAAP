Is TAAP really free for therapists?

Yes. And if you bring your clients onto TAAP during the trial period, you keep lifetime free access to the full platform and ecosystem — permanently.

Will this be complicated for my clients to use?

No. Your client doesn't set anything up — you do. The moment you enroll them, they see only what you've assigned: their sessions, their activities, their reminders. No blank screen, no settings to configure, no learning curve. If they can send a text message, they can use this.
